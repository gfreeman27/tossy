Tossy: Photo Cleaner

Swipe through your camera roll one photo at a time. Keep it, favorite it, or toss it. Everything stays on your iPhone.

How it works

  • Swipe right to keep, up to favorite, left to add to your To Delete folder.
  • Discover finds duplicates, similar shots, screenshots and soft-focus photos for you to review.
  • To Delete lets you double-check, then delete the whole batch at once. Deleted photos go to Recently Deleted in Photos for 30 days.
  • Pick from 50 app icons in Settings.

Support

Questions, bugs or feature requests? Email gfreeman27@aim.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn’t working.

Common questions

Can I get a deleted photo back? Yes. Open the Photos app, go to Recently Deleted, and recover it within 30 days.

Tossy can’t see all my photos. You may have given limited access. Go to Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos › Tossy and choose Full Access.

Does Tossy upload my photos? No. Tossy has no servers and never uploads anything. See the privacy policy.
