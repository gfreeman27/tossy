Tossy

Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

Tossy: Photo Cleaner (“Tossy”) helps you review and clear out photos on your iPhone. Tossy does not collect, store, sell or share any of your personal data. Everything the app does happens on your device.

Your photos

Tossy asks for permission to access your photo library so you can review your photos and decide what to keep, favorite or delete. You can grant full or limited access, and change it any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos.

  • Your photos never leave your device because of Tossy. Tossy has no servers and never uploads, copies or transmits your photos or information about them.
  • Suggestions in Discover (such as duplicates, similar shots, screenshots and soft-focus photos) are worked out entirely on your iPhone using Apple’s on-device frameworks.
  • If your library uses iCloud Photos, Apple’s Photos framework may download a full-size copy of a photo from your own iCloud account so it can be shown. That transfer is handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy; Tossy never sees or receives it.
  • Tossy only deletes photos when you ask it to. Each deletion is confirmed by an iOS system prompt, and deleted photos go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where you can recover them for up to 30 days.
  • When you favorite a photo in Tossy, it is marked as a favorite in your Photos library.

Information stored on your device

To let you pick up where you left off, Tossy saves your review progress (which photos you have kept, favorited or added to your To Delete folder) and your chosen app icon. This information is stored only on your iPhone, is never sent anywhere, and is removed when you delete the app.

What Tossy does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party SDKs.
  • No collection of your name, email, location, contacts or device identifiers.
  • No sale or sharing of data with anyone.

Children

Tossy does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If Tossy ever begins collecting data, the policy will be updated before that happens.
